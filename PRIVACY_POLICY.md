# Privacy Policy for AswitchI

**Effective Date:** August 25, 2026
**Provider:** Lorapok Labs

## 1. Overview
AswitchI is a privacy-first, locally-run Linux desktop application. We believe that your engineering data and AI prompts belong to you.

## 2. Data Collection
AswitchI **does not** collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII), analytics, telemetry, or usage data to Lorapok Labs or any third-party servers.

## 3. Local Execution
All core functionality, including the macOS-style Launchpad, the 3D Dock, and the application scanning engine, operates entirely locally on your Ubuntu/Linux machine. 

## 4. Third-Party Web AI Services
AswitchI acts as an isolated WebView (via WebKit2GTK) for accessing third-party AI services (e.g., ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini). 
* When you log into these services through AswitchI, your session data (cookies, local storage) is stored locally on your hard drive.
* Your interactions with these services are governed solely by their respective Privacy Policies (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic, Google). Lorapok Labs does not intercept, read, or have access to your prompts or responses.
