ENGINEERING EXCELLENCE

Why Developers Switch to AswitchI

Engineered without Electron bloat for instant response times and deep Linux desktop synergy.

Native WebKit2GTK Engine

Built in Python 3 with direct GObject Introspection. Consumes less than 45MB RAM while offering full hardware-accelerated 60fps animations.

Encrypted AI Web Sessions

Zero session loss. Persistent WebKit cookie vaults for Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini keep you logged in without polluting your primary web browser.

Fuzzy Deep Indexing

Autodetects Cursor, Windsurf, VS Code, Cline, Aider, Ollama, and local models across standard paths and custom virtual environments.

Multi-Monitor macOS Dock

Fluid auto-hiding dock with magnification physics, top-deck toast notifications, and customizable corner radii that seamlessly match Ubuntu themes.
